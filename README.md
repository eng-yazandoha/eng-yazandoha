## Philosophy

I don't enjoy building software for the sake of writing code.

I enjoy solving business problems, simplifying workflows,
and turning ideas into products that people actually use.

Over the last 4 years I have been focused on building:

* SaaS Platforms
* Business Management Systems
* POS & Cashier Solutions
* Mobile Applications
* Internal Company Tools
* Automation Systems

Every project starts with understanding the problem,
then designing the system,
and only then writing code.
